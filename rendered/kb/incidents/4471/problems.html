Fixing the parser does not close #4471. The form hint and the one-day move stay broken after it ships.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug the #4471 page describes, and most of its reports.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. It prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field for every tenant. People follow the hint, and the form reads what they typed the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap, but the fix is in the form, and fixing the parser alone does not touch it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here.

one-day: the date moves by one day. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. No day/month order explains that. The desk already noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but the cause table filed them under parser by tenant. They are few, and likely under-reported: one reporter says they stopped reporting them.

How to tell them apart

  • Go by the tenant, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts), then by what the body shows. A swap names two dates with day and month exchanged; a one-day move does not.
  • Not by intakeTag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the table above counts how often that guess points somewhere else.
  • Not by subject line. Decision 0001 merges on subject, and all three problems arrive under the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”).
